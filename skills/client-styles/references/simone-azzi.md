## Structure
- **Context first, hooks second** [Simone, C03a–d and C03h feedback, Nov 2026]: she said the v1s were confusing ("someone watching this won't get it"). Cause: cold hooks lifted from mid-answer, setup lines cut, and lines from different minutes spliced so "it", "because" or "25 years ago" pointed at something unsaid. Fix that she approved: play each answer **in source order**, starting on the setup line (e.g. "When you're sitting opposite an agent at your dining table…", "Marketing 101, let's break it down…", "What I find really fascinating is going back to 25 years ago…").
- Reorder only whole thoughts, and only when the new order is clearer (C03h: market → why the choice matters → what happens if you get it wrong). Never splice mid-sentence fragments from different minutes.
- No cold-open teaser that repeats the ending: on C03h it jumped from strategy to "dog eat dog" and Simone found it made no sense [Simone, C03h, 9 Nov 2026].
- Spell out who and why: viewers must hear that it's about agents and their motives (C03h: "agents are fighting each other for listings… not promising some inflated dream price just to win the business") [Simone, C03h].
- Tim dropped "dog eat dog" and "I can't tell you" openings in his finals [Tim, C03g/C03h, 11 Nov 2026].
