# Namir Mikha style profile

Tags: [Tim] confirmed by Tim · [inferred] measured from his finals, not confirmed · ask = unknown.
Sources: C6 "Full-Circle Story", C1, C7 (Simon), learned 14-15 Oct 2026. Details: project docs "Tim edit lessons C7", "C7 Simon edit notes", "Tim colour grading lessons", "Tim edit system page".

## Client and footage
- Who: Namir Mikha, real estate agent. Concepts feature Namir, vendors, builders and other guests (e.g. Simon on C7). [Tim]
- Concept types: numbered concept videos (C1, C6 "Full-Circle Story", C7 build story). [Tim]
- Footage: Dropbox `Namir Mikha/[01. Content]/Videography/1. SHOOTS/2026/Shoot <n>. <street>/`, with `_shotlog/` beside the cards. Never write media there. [Tim]
- Interviews: single camera, punch-ins for angle changes. [inferred, C7]

## Format and length
- Delivery: vertical 1080x1920, 24p. [Tim]
- Length: about 1 min, flexible. Ask per concept. [Tim]

## Structure
- Open with 1-2 s of B-roll before the first line, so the audience gets where they are. [Tim, C6]
- Beats from the brief; C7 ran: first step → approach and outcome → materials, finishes and design decisions → conclusion. [inferred, C7]
- Breathing gap of hero property shots, music only, between two points or after a long fast stretch. C7's grew from 2.5 s to 4.4 s with 3 hero shots, sized to the music. [Tim / C7]
- Walk-and-talk concepts keep the interviewer's questions in. [Tim]

## A-roll
- Remove silent mid-sentence gaps of about 0.3 s or more; keep phrasing pauses. C7 speech went from 80.1 s to 74.0 s. [Tim, C7]
- Air: about 0.10 s before the first word and 0.15 s after the last, about 0.28 s across a join. [inferred, C7]
- Punch-ins alternate Zoom 1.0 and 1.35, conclusion in the close-up. C7: medium Zoom 1.0 Pan −40; close-up Zoom 1.35 Pan −54 Tilt −150. [inferred, C7]
- Jump cuts may stay uncovered when the head moves under about 1% of the frame, or when covering would leave a sliver. [Tim, C7]
- Jokes: ask per concept; default keep the speaker on camera. [Tim]

## B-roll
- Coverage about 50/50; walkthrough pieces run heavier (C7 64%). Open each speaker turn on camera 1.5-2 s. [Tim / C7]
- Speeds: 50p empty-house shots at 48% (down to ~30% to hide shake); people at 50-60%. [Tim]
- Match literally: show what's named, and the person being talked about. A line about a specific room or light shows that room. [Tim]
- Goofy people moments only under light lines (Namir on the Pilates reformer under a serious line was wrong). [Tim, C6]
- Avoid: blown hot spots as the subject, weak static shots held long (C7 R14), videographer move resets (C6 0334). [Tim]
- Prefer shots not used in other Namir concepts. [Tim]

## Audio and music
- Dialogue: Tim exports, runs speech enhancement, puts it on A2 and disables A1 (speech 50% for Namir, 37% for Simon, background 10%, music 10%). Don't touch A2 or re-enable A1 after that. [Tim / C7]
- Music: Tim's pass. Start the song so its lift lands on a story beat (C7: band in at frame 290, beat 2 at 295); break over a phrase change; end card on a phrase start. [inferred, C7]
- Loudness target: ask.

## Ending and brand
- B-roll carries about 4 s past the last word over music, starting just before speech ends. [inferred, C7]
- Standard tail card `[03. Assets]/Logos/Namir Reel Tail_Standard.png`, 65 frames, music running about 0.6 s past it. Same on C6, C1, C7. [inferred, repeated]

## Grade
- Tim's 9-node template; only Balance (Temp, Tint) and HDR Global change. Neutrals about Cb −5, Cr +5; main white surface about 896. [Tim]
- Grade by scene: one hero shot per room and light, copied to the rest. C7: six interiors share one white balance. [Tim]
- Interview A-roll keeps the template unless the light changes in the take (C1 C0834 varied Temp −10 to −170 between segments). [inferred, C1]
- Warm-lit bedrooms stay slightly warm; hero facades keep some warmth. [Tim]
