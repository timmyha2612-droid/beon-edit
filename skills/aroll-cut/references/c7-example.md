## What Tim changed and why

1. **Pause tightening inside sentences.**
   - He removed 8 silent gaps of 0.17–0.42 s, all pure silence (e.g. "dramatically | light-filled, | large living areas").
   - Speech time went from 80.1 s to 74.0 s.
   - Pauses of 0.3–0.5 s are kept only where they read as natural phrasing.
2. **Less air at joins.**
   - Before the first word: about 0.10 s (mine 0.16).
   - After the last word: about 0.15 s (mine 0.24).
   - Total silence across a join: about 0.28 s (mine 0.42).
3. **Tone rule, refined.**
   - A continuing tone at a cut is fine when the next piece continues the same sentence.
   - It's only wrong when the next piece starts a new thought (the C1 problem).
   - My "falling tone + 0.3 s pause only" rule was too strict for joins inside a sentence.
4. **Uncovered jump cuts are fine in two cases (Tim):**
   - The audio flows and the jump isn't significant. Measured: head moves under 1% of the frame across the cut (5 such cuts: 328, 980, 1006, 1117, 1693). The jumps he covered moved 2–3× more.
   - Covering it would leave a very short sliver of the talking head between two B-roll shots.
5. **Dropped R14 (0247).**
   - Tim: it's a weak shot. There's literally a light spot in it, and it lingers too long, so it feels awkward.
   - Rule: reject shots where a blown hot spot is the subject, and don't hold a weak static shot long. R14 was my longest shot at 3.5 s.
   - Side effect: Simon stays on camera for 4 s on "the lighting and the material finishes speaks for itself".
6. **R4 slipped 0.56 s later in the clip,** toward its steadier part. It was flagged "shake CHECK 0.39"; the same clip measured 0.15 later in the clip when I used it for C1.
7. **Music built around the story ("Lucky Seven", In at 11.8 s of the song):**
   - The full band comes in at frame 290, just as beat 2 starts at 295 ("From the very initial concept").
   - The break grew from 2.5 s to 4.4 s, with 3 hero shots (R16 moved into it, then G1, G2). It sits over the song's deepest mid-track dip at 1428.
   - The A-roll stops on a beat (1369), and G2 overlaps the returning speech by 3 frames.
   - The end card lands on the new phrase right after the music dip at 2004.
8. **Outro.**
   - B-roll carries past the last word: R20 starts 5 frames before the end of speech, then OUT, about 4.4 s of picture over music.
   - Then the standard Namir end card, "Namir Reel Tail_Standard.png" (65 frames, same on C6 and C1).
   - The music runs about 0.6 s past the card.
   - OUT 0329 is at Zoom 1.33. It was already like that this morning, before Tim's edit, and neither of us remembers setting it, so it's not a rule.
9. **Dialogue audio.**
   - A1 is disabled. An enhanced dialogue file sits on A2.
   - The filename suggests enhanced-speech settings: speech 37% for Simon (50% on Namir's C6/C1), background 10%, music 10%.
   - The music is on A3.

## Rules to apply next time

- Remove silent mid-sentence gaps of about 0.3 s or more; keep natural phrasing pauses. Leave about 0.10 s before the first word and about 0.15 s after the last.
- Cover visible jump cuts. Leave invisible ones (head movement under 1%), and leave any where covering would create a tiny on-camera sliver.
- B-roll: no shots built around a blown light spot, and keep weak static shots short.
- When music is in: start the song so its lift lands on a story beat, place the break over a musical phrase change and size it to fit, and land the end card on a phrase start.
- Every Namir reel ends with the standard tail card (65 frames) after the outro shot, with the music running slightly past it.
